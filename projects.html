Lingalo

A modern language learning platform engineered for script mastery, spaced repetition, and gamified practice.

Lingalo provides an intuitive, zero-distraction environment to master international alphabets and essential vocabulary. It pairs systematic spaced repetition (SRS) algorithms with interactive review sessions, audio pronunciation, and continuous habit tracking.

Supported languages & writing systems

  • Japanese Hiragana & Katakana
  • Korean Hangul Script
  • Spanish Vocabulary & Grammar
  • French Core Phrases
  • German Articles & Lexicon
Spaced Repetition (SRS)
Automated review intervals calculated to optimize recall and cement vocabulary into permanent memory.
Script Mastery Modes
Targeted character drills and recognition sessions for complex Asian writing systems.
Audio Word Bank
Curated vocabulary lists paired with native sound effects and audio pronunciation clips.
Streaks & Leveling
Daily practice streaks, XP milestones, and mastery progress meters to sustain learning momentum.

LingaloKR

A more in-depth version of Lingalo, focused entirely on Korean.

LingaloKR takes the Lingalo approach deeper for a single language. It covers Hangul, both Korean number systems, and hundreds of everyday words with example sentences, and schedules each word for review so it sticks.

What's covered

  • Hangul Consonants & Vowels
  • Numbers Sino-Korean & Native
  • Vocabulary Everyday Words & Phrases
  • Class Lessons 336 Curriculum Words
Daily Reviews
Every word gets its own review date, so each day starts with exactly what's due.
Number Drills
Practice prices, time, dates, ages, and counting with freshly generated questions.
Listening Practice
Hear a word in Korean and pick its meaning to train your ear.
Progress Tracking
An activity chart, practice calendar, streaks, and levels.

Streak Lab

A photo editor for pixel-streak art, light and motion effects.

Streak Lab pulls the pixels of a photo into streaks: in straight lines, rings, star-trail swirls, out of edges or along curved paths. Layers, masks and an on-device AI subject finder control where they go. Light adjustments, a tone curve, film-style halation, risograph print and other effects finish the look, then it exports full-size photos, Instagram carousels and Reels. A second tool, Portals, puts photos in windows on each other: split windows across two backgrounds, swapped squares and cross splits. Both run entirely in the browser, keep their work on the device, and work offline once installed.

Streak modes

  • Vertical Streaks rise from a line
  • Radial Rings around a centre point
  • Edge Out of edges you draw
  • Swirl & Path Star trails and curved streaks
  • Layers & masks Combine modes, limit them to areas
Protect & Erase Brushes
Paint over the subject to keep it sharp, and trim edges back where the streaks shouldn't go.
Light & Effects
Exposure, tone curve and colour, then halation, light leaks, echo trails, slit-scan, risograph, dither and more, finished with a duotone grade and grain.
Instagram Exports
1080 × 1350 carousel slides, plus Reel, Story and feed videos in eight animation styles, from growing streaks to glitch reveals and beat-matched cuts.
Full Resolution
Saves at the photo's original size, including photos over 16 megapixels.

Azimuth

An open-source intelligence workbench that runs entirely in the browser: paste anything, see what's public about it, and turn the findings into a report.

Azimuth identifies whatever you paste (a username, email, domain, IP address, phone number, file hash, crypto wallet or name), checks free public sources straight away, and summarises what it found in plain English. New leads it uncovers can be followed with one click and appear on a link chart, map and timeline. Evidence is graded, hashed and timestamped, and cases export as law-enforcement, journalism or due-diligence reports. There is no server and no account: data stays in the browser and can be locked with a passphrase.

What it investigates

  • People Usernames, emails, phones, names
  • Infrastructure Domains, IPs, URLs, lookalikes
  • Media Photos, video, documents
  • Money Wallets, companies, sanctions
  • Directory 750 health-checked tools
Live Checks & Leads
DNS, registration, certificate logs, archives, open ports, threat intel and account checks run automatically, and anything new they uncover becomes a lead.
Analyst Tradecraft
Intelligence requirements, Admiralty source grading, competing-hypotheses analysis, standard probability language and peer review.
Evidence Integrity
SHA-256 hashes, Bitcoin-anchored timestamps, archived copies, redaction with a sealed original, and a full audit trail.
Works With Pro Tools
Exports to Maltego, i2 Analyst's Notebook, STIX, MISP and CASE/UCO; imports Sherlock, Maigret, SpiderFoot and more.
