ijme.tk/lingalo

Lingalo ↗

A modern language learning platform engineered for script mastery, spaced repetition, and gamified practice.

Lingalo provides an intuitive, zero-distraction environment to master international alphabets and essential vocabulary. It pairs systematic spaced repetition (SRS) algorithms with interactive review sessions, audio pronunciation, and continuous habit tracking.
Supported Languages & Writing Systems
🇯🇵 Japanese (Hiragana & Katakana) 🇰🇷 Korean (Hangul Script) 🇪🇸 Spanish (Vocabulary & Grammar) 🇫🇷 French (Core Phrases) 🇩🇪 German (Articles & Lexicon)
⚡ Spaced Repetition (SRS)

Automated review intervals calculated to optimize recall and cement vocabulary into permanent memory.

✍️ Script Mastery Modes

Targeted character drills and recognition sessions for complex Asian writing systems.

📚 Audio Word Bank

Curated vocabulary lists paired with native sound effects and audio pronunciation clips.

🔥 Streaks & Leveling

Daily practice streaks, XP milestones, and mastery progress meters to sustain learning momentum.
